Publications

Fake Face Recognition on Images Generated by Various Deepfake Tools

This paper evaluates the effectiveness of popular deepfake generation tools (Deepfakes, Face2Face, Face Swap, and Neural Textures) in deceiving deep learning–based fake/real face recognition systems. Experimental results indicate that Neural Textures produces the most challenging fake faces for detection algorithms. Additionally, the study proposes a lightweight deep learning architecture that achieves improved detection performance with fewer parameters.

View publication on Springer

An Approach for Object Recognition in Videos for Vocabulary Extraction

This study presents a method to support English vocabulary learning through real-time object recognition in videos, images, and live streams. The approach utilizes YOLO-based deep learning architectures to detect objects and generate related vocabulary, meanings, and example sentences. Experimental results achieve an mAP of 55.6 on a dataset of 2,786 images across 59 classes, demonstrating practical applicability for language learning.
